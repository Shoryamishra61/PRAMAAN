# Annotator Guidelines: Independent Dispute Verification

**Standard:** Section 60–62 of Final Directive  
**Status:** Protocol Frozen  

---

## 1. Role of Annotators

Annotators independently review the evidence packet created by authors and determine:
1. Is the evidence packet internally consistent?
2. Are all required documents present?
3. Where does a contradiction occur? (Highlight exact text span in customer note vs ledger entry).

---

## 2. Dual-Annotation & Disagreement Tracking

- A minimum of 20% of all cases (100/500 cases) must be annotated by two independent annotators.
- Inter-annotator agreement is computed using **Cohen's Kappa ($\kappa$)** on binary consistency and **Token F1** on contradiction span grounding.
- Cases where annotators disagree proceed to the formal adjudication committee.
