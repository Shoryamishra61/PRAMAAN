## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Formulation

In accordance with Track 02 (**AI Risk Manager: Stop the merchant losing money to fraud, returns and chargebacks**), the problem wedge is frozen:
$$\text{Input: Dispute Evidence Packet} \longrightarrow \text{Output: Calibrated Risk, Grounded Contradictions, Formal Invariant Proof, Action} \in \{\text{PASS}, \text{REVIEW}, \text{BLOCK}\}$$

Because genuine, open-access, end-to-end chargeback dossiers containing raw dispute claims, merchant evidence, and authoritative processor ledgers do not exist in the public domain due to banking secrecy (GLBA, PCI-DSS, RBI DPDP Act), **FECL-Bench V2** establishes a 4-tier transparent scientific architecture.

### Tier A: FECL-SCM-V2 (Structural Causal Simulator)
- **Scale:** 120,000 total cases across 8 immutable partitions:
  - `TRAIN`: 70,000 cases (Canonical G0, Varied G1, Hinglish G2)
  - `VALIDATION`: 10,000 cases (Hyperparameter & model selection)
  - `CALIBRATION`: 10,000 cases (Temperature scaling & conformal risk envelope)
  - `FINAL_TEST`: 10,000 cases (Frozen IID evaluation)
  - `TEMPLATE_HOLDOUT`: 5,000 cases (Unseen surface phrasing G3)
  - `MECHANISM_HOLDOUT`: 5,000 cases (Unseen compositional contradiction types)
  - `DISTRIBUTION_SHIFT`: 5,000 cases (Severe OCR noise, currency shifts)
  - `OOD_OPEN_SET`: 5,000 cases (Unseen dispute categories, open-set tokens)
- **Domain Grounding:** Built directly from Razorpay's dispute ontology:
  - `CREDIT_NOT_PROCESSED`: Refund promised or partial refund issued vs customer claiming full refund.
  - `GOODS_SERVICES_NOT_RECEIVED`: Carrier tracking states "Label Created" while merchant claims delivered.
  - `GOODS_SERVICES_NOT_AS_DESCRIBED`: SKU and variant mismatches between invoice and customer receipt.
  - `DUPLICATE_CHARGE`: Two identical authorizations vs legitimate recurring subscription.
  - `AUTHORIZATION_ERROR`: Currency mismatch or capture exceeding authorized amount.
- **Point-in-Time Discipline:** Every event logs `event_time`, `available_time`, `ingestion_time`, and `decision_time`. Strict causal invariant:
  $$\text{available\_time} \le \text{decision\_time}$$

### Tier B: Public Financial Priors & Temporal Simulators
- **Federal Reserve CardSim (FEDS 2025-017):** 1,000,000 transactions modeled via agent-based Bayesian simulation of consumer payment choices. Provides realistic baseline transaction distributions, amount frequencies, and merchant category codes (MCC).
- **Fraud Detection Handbook Simulator:** 1.75M transactions providing temporal walk-forward backtest scenarios with non-stationary drift and realistic class imbalance.

### Tier C: Public Document & Receipt Robustness
- **CORD (Consolidated Receipt Dataset):** 1,000 receipts with token-level bounding boxes and key-information annotations. Evaluates line-item price extraction, tax extraction, and date parsing under real physical receipts.
- **SROIE (ICDAR 2019):** 1,000 scanned receipts for optical character recognition and key information extraction under physical optical scan degradation.

### Tier D: Real Financial Language & Independent Challenge
- **CFPB Consumer Complaint Narratives:** Historical snapshot of real-world consumer dispute correspondence. Used strictly for financial complaint vocabulary diversity and long-form narrative stress; **never** treated as ground-truth chargeback fraud labels.
- **FECL-CROSSGEN-5K:** 5,000 cases with latent financial truth generated from the causal simulator, but surface text generated by an independent generator unseen during training.
- **Independent Human-Authored Challenge Pack:** Multi-author blinded protocol (5 independent authors, 100 cases each). Maintained under `STATUS = PENDING_EXTERNAL_VALIDATION` until executed with external human annotators.

## 3. Ground Truth & Oracle Semantics

1. **State-First, Language-Second:** The underlying structural financial state $(\text{Authorization}, \text{Capture}, \text{Refunds}, \text{Carrier})$ is generated first. Textual representations are rendered conditionally from the ground truth state.
2. **Oracle Grounding:** Contradiction labels are mathematically derived from structural state comparisons and formal SMT constraints, never by querying an LLM as a pseudo-oracle.
3. **Multi-Task Targets:**
   - `is_financially_consistent` (Binary classification)
   - `contradiction_type` (Categorical: amount mismatch, chronology violation, status conflict, cumulative over-refund)
   - `evidence_sufficiency` (Sufficient, Incomplete, Missing Critical Document)
   - `missing_critical_evidence` (Ledger, Carrier Proof, Customer Note)
   - `formal_invariant_status` (SAT, UNSAT, INCOMPLETE)
   - `ground_truth_core_nodes` & `ground_truth_core_edges` (Minimal Contradiction Certificate)

---

## 4. Leakage & Contamination Controls

1. **Zero Entity Collision:** Merchant IDs, customer IDs, and transaction IDs are partitioned strictly by split.
2. **Zero Template Collision:** Template families used in `TEMPLATE_HOLDOUT` and `FECL-CROSSGEN-5K` are cryptographically barred from the `TRAIN` generator.
3. **Point-in-Time Backtest Firewall:** In temporal evaluations, cases evaluated at time $t$ strictly use historical data available at $t' \le t$.
4. **Final-Test One-Shot Access:** Frozen test partitions are touched only once after model and policy hyperparameters are locked.

---

## 5. Explicit Limitations & Scientific Truth

1. **Synthetic-to-Real Gap:** While FECL-SCM-V2 models realistic dispute workflows, real merchant environments feature subtle informal communication, emotional venting, and merchant-specific accounting practices that synthetic generators approximate.
2. **No Claim of Magic Real Data:** We do not claim access to proprietary internal bank databases. We demonstrate that combining inductive financial priors with formal verification achieves high sample efficiency on controlled data.
