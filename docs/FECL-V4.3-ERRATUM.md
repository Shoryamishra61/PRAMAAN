# FECL-Bench v4.3 metadata and acquisition erratum

Status: final benchmark candidate before model fitting and TEST access.

The v4.2 files are preserved but invalid because their row-level `benchmark_id`, case IDs,
parser versions, and source URIs retained v4 identifiers even though the directory manifest said
v4.2. No model was fitted and TEST was not evaluated.

v4.3 applies the v4.2 causal and grounding corrections, makes every row self-identify as v4.3,
and makes acquisition trajectories terminal under the label-blind compiler. For special
reference, order, and policy contradictions, the contradiction-specific resolver may terminate
without acquiring unrelated refund state; consistent cases must still acquire refund state before
PASS eligibility. Oracle fields remain evaluator-only and are forbidden model/compiler inputs.

The split membership, family isolation, counts, costs, and original TEST-access protocol are
unchanged.
