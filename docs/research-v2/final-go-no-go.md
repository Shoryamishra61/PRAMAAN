# Final go / no-go

## Decision

- **DESIGN DECISION — GO:** Continue Dispute Integrity Gate as a narrow, read-only `refund_not_processed_v1` financial evidence debugger.
- **DESIGN DECISION — GO:** Preserve the current deterministic runtime and interactive evidence -> finding -> decision -> repair loop.
- **DESIGN DECISION — GO:** Begin a separately versioned real-data research program for grounded span extraction and pairwise relation detection.
- **DESIGN DECISION — NO-GO:** Do not pivot to general fraud scoring, return-risk, abuse-ring detection or autonomous representment without proprietary labels/data and a new product thesis.
- **DESIGN DECISION — NO-GO:** Do not promote TF-IDF, MiniLM, XGBoost or NLI from v1; the saved results do not meet the safety standard.
- **DESIGN DECISION — NO-GO:** Do not train LoRA/SFT or a GNN until the corresponding label/topology gates in the tournament are met.
- **DESIGN DECISION — NO-GO:** Do not claim formal conformal risk control, production impact, win-rate lift or uniqueness from the present synthetic study.

## Why this survives

- **FACT:** The selected loss class maps directly to Razorpay's RZP04/refund evidence guidance.
- **RESEARCH RESULT:** Competitor and patent research kills evidence automation, win prediction and graph fraud as novelty claims, but does not eliminate a demonstrable pre-submit integrity debugger and human repair contract.
- **RESEARCH RESULT:** v1 proves the rules baseline is strong on DEV and weak on unseen paraphrase while learned models introduce material semantic/grounding errors; this is an honest, consequential research problem.
- **DESIGN DECISION:** The product has value even if ML loses: deterministic reconciliation and safe REVIEW/BLOCK behavior remain useful and judge-visible.

## Next gates

2. **DESIGN DECISION:** Freeze research-v2 hypotheses, split manifests and cost grid before model fitting.

5. **DESIGN DECISION:** Shadow any promoted extractor; deterministic policy remains the authority.

## Judge-safe claim

> **DESIGN DECISION:** Dispute Integrity Gate is a working, defense-only evidence debugger for refund-not-processed disputes. It learns only to identify grounded claims, reconciles financial truth deterministically, abstains on uncertainty, and makes every decision change traceable to evidence. Current learned challengers are shown honestly as rejected or research-only; production effectiveness is not yet established.

## Readiness verdict

- **FACT:** Product/demo readiness: `GO` for a synthetic, local Buildathon demonstration with existing disclosures.
- **FACT:** Learned-model deployment readiness: `NO-GO` based on v1 promotion failures.
- **UNVERIFIED:** Merchant loss reduction and analyst-time improvement are not yet measured on real cases.
- **DESIGN DECISION:** Research-v2 readiness is `CONDITIONAL GO` only after the data/consent/label gate.
