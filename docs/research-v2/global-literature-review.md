# Global literature review

## Method

- **FACT:** This review prioritizes peer-reviewed conference/journal pages, official proceedings, and author-hosted papers; arXiv is used when a stronger accessible source was unavailable.
- **DESIGN DECISION:** Results from transaction-fraud datasets are not transferred to dispute-evidence integrity without a new experiment because the unit, labels and loss are different.

## Synthesis

- **RESEARCH RESULT:** Evidence-aware NLP supports joint evaluation of the predicted relation and the exact evidence span; this aligns with the product's grounded-claim contract.
- **RESEARCH RESULT:** Graph-fraud gains arise when cross-entity topology contains information; the current per-case evidence graph has typed relations but no demonstrated statistical sample of graph structures.
- **RESEARCH RESULT:** Calibration, selective prediction and conformal risk control address different questions; calibration quality alone neither proves OOD safety nor controls a critical error rate.
- **DESIGN DECISION:** The next research contribution is a grounded relation model plus a separate deterministic/risk controller, not an end-to-end model that owns the financial decision.
