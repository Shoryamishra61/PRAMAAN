## Acceptance tests

- default screen accepts editable raw reason code, INR amount, customer communication, refund
  state/amount, and ledger completeness;
- submit performs a real local `POST /api/v1/sandbox/evaluate` and never substitutes client-side
  decision logic;
- backend converts exact-decimal INR strings to integer minor units, runs `RegexBaselineExtractor`,
  grounds the exact quote/span, and invokes the existing deterministic verifier/decision policy;
- no refund record plus a grounded processed-refund claim returns BLOCK and
  `F_REFUND_CLAIM_NO_LEDGER_MATCH`;
- matching processed refund returns PASS;
- incomplete trusted ledger returns REVIEW and `F_STRUCTURED_STATE_INCOMPLETE`;
- raw `reason_code` is preserved verbatim and never drives an invented network-code mapping;
- response proves local/offline, ephemeral, synthetic, not persisted, no holdout access, no external
  call, and no Razorpay write;
- floats, unknown fields, blank/invalid money, and inconsistent refund state/amount are rejected;
- output visibly exposes the grounded quote, span, typed claim, normalized ledger, rule, and state.
